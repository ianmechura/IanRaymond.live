IAN RAYMOND — ROOT PASS

1. Open PlayIanRaymond.html in your web browser.
2. Click PLAY ALBUM, or choose a song.

The music and included press kit work without an internet connection.
Website, YouTube and TikTok links need internet.

Keep the complete folder together. You can copy it to your computer and
open PlayIanRaymond.html there, too.

Prefer your own music app? Open music/Root Pass.m3u, or any MP3 in music/.

If a track does not play, select another track and check your audio output.
The music folder is also available if your browser cannot run the player.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls; Enter/Space activates a focused
button. Arrow keys adjust the seek and volume sliders. Space toggles
play/pause when the page background has keyboard focus.

To update files, close the player tab, copy in the replacements, then
reopen PlayIanRaymond.html. See CONTENT GUIDE.md for details.
